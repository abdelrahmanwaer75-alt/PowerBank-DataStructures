# Requirements
- PowerBank records: ID, status, station.
- Station records: ID, name, total and available devices.
- Reservation records and FIFO waiting list.
- Rental records and status-change history.
- BST: insert, search, update, delete, traversal.
- Queue: enqueue, dequeue, peek, isEmpty, display.
- Linked list: insert, search, update, delete, traverse.
- Sorting: PowerBanks by ID, stations by availability, reservations/reports by date/status.
- Required submissions: design, runnable program, at least five test cases with expected/actual results and traces, technical report, demonstration.
